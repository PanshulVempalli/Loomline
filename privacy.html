Privacy notice

Last updated 23 November 2026

Who we are

Loomline is run by Panshul Vempalli, London, and is the controller of your personal information under UK data protection law. Contact: panshulvarma@gmail.com.

What we collect and why

If you send the audit form or email us: your name, business name, email address, website link and any message you write. We use it to prepare your free website audit, reply to you, and discuss whether we can help. Our lawful basis is taking steps at your request, and our legitimate interest in running a small business.

If you book a call: the details you give the booking service, so we can hold the call with you.

If we contact your business first: we may email or message a business using contact details it publishes itself, about something specific we noticed on its website. We only email limited companies and similar businesses, we say who we are, and every message tells you how to stop us. Our lawful basis is legitimate interests. We do not buy contact lists.

Cookies and tracking

This website does not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers, and fonts are served from our own site. If that changes, we will ask for your consent first and update this page.

Who else handles your information

We use a small number of providers that act on our behalf: Vercel (website hosting), Web3Forms (delivers audit requests to our inbox), Google (Gmail, for email) and Zapier (connects our email to our tools). We choose providers that do not use your information for their own marketing, and we do not use it to train AI models. Some of these are based in the United States, and we rely on the safeguards they provide for international transfers. We do not sell your information.

If you tell us health or other sensitive details

Please do not put health or other sensitive details in the audit form or in any message to us. We only need business contact details. If you send something sensitive by mistake, tell us and we will delete it.

How long we keep it

If we do not work together, we delete your audit request and messages within 12 months. If we do work together, we keep records for as long as the law requires, usually six years for accounts.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your information, object to how we use it, or ask us to stop contacting you. Email panshulvarma@gmail.com and we will reply within one month. If you are unhappy, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Changes

We will update this page and the date above if our practices change.
