Five checks. One honest summary.

We look at your current website, or your Google Business Profile if you do not have a site yet, and tell you what works, what does not and what to fix first. Use it yourself, with another developer or with us.

Get a free 5-point website audit

Mobile experience

Is it easy to read, scroll and tap on a phone, where most local searches happen?

Speed

Does the page load quickly on an ordinary mobile connection?

Local findability

Do titles, headings and local details help you appear for the work you want in your area?

Contact path

Can a visitor call, book or enquire in one or two taps, and does the form actually work?

Trust and accessibility basics

Is it readable, usable by keyboard and clear about who you are?

Do you only work in London?

London is our focus, so we can talk things through in person if that helps. Remote work is fine too.

Is the audit really free?

Yes. You get a short written summary of what we found. We will mention how we could help, but there is no pressure and no obligation.

Will an AI assistant replace me or my staff?

No. It handles the repetitive first questions and takes down job details so you can reply faster. A person always handles the real work and the final decisions.
